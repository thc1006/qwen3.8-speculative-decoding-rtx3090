============================================================================================

  NOT AN IDLE MEASUREMENT, and left in the table rather than dropped: model-idle at 7 s. The server had just been
  started and the card was still coming down from loading 16 GB of weights; the
  windows opened 5 s after the health check passed, which was not enough. Its mean
  power says so -- twice the idle floor -- and its offset has the other sign.

  Over the 49 windows that are idle: the offset is +0.499 W, spread 0.308, and the JOULES scale with the window while
  the WATTS do not. So it is a level difference between the two readout paths, not
  a per-window quantity -- and not a filter, because no linear filter can put
  anything at all on a window whose two ends sit at the same level.

  WHAT THIS DOES NOT ESTABLISH. It is measured at the idle floor, about 28 W, and
  a resident model does not raise that floor: a server holding 16 GB and answering
  nothing sits at the same 28 W as a bare card. Phase E5's windows idle at about
  128 W between requests, which is therefore NOT steady idle but a card still on
  its way down, and this measurement says nothing about the offset there. Against
  it, `results/phase_e.json`'s 150 W arms hold a nearly flat trace for 44.8 s and
  show 0.596 J of offset in total, which bounds the difference at that level to
  about 0.013 W. Whatever this is, it is large at the floor and small under load,
  and the levels in between are not measured.
