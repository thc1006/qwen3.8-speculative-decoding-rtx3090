--- TEST 1: is the overhead charged to REJECTION? ---
    Model a rejection-proportional cost r (decode-steps per rejected draft token):
        k = k_verify + r * n_max * (1 - acceptance)
        => dk/d(acceptance) = -r * n_max,   so  r = -slope / n_max
    A state-rollback account of the overhead predicts r > 0, i.e. slope < 0.

  Two methods with independent drafters fitted separately. Agreement in `c` with
  a difference in `k0` is consistent with the marginal cost sitting outside the
  drafter, but it does not identify it. What `k` measures is the whole speculative
  cycle: target verification, the drafter's own forwards, sampling, launch and
  synchronisation, output extraction and any per-step state management. The two
  methods share every one of those except the drafter, so a shared slope narrows
  the cost to that shared machinery without saying which part of it. Separating
  them needs per-context CUDA-event timing, a replay that skips drafter compute,
  or a profiler decomposition - none of which this file has.
