  A NEGATIVE 'moved' is convergence on the counter. If the instantaneous
  integral converges while the averaged one does not, the averaged field
  is the under-resolved reading and the offset is real. If the
  instantaneous one diverges, it is the grid, and the -36.3 % correction
  in Correction 44 rests on an artefact.
